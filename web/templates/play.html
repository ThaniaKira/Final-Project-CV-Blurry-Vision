🎵 MUSIC & VOICE

Choose a friendly installed English voice, then tap “Hear it again” anytime. Memory Trail reads the whole sequence twice. Original instrumental music is optional.

🧠 TWO TRAINED MODELS

Counting, Math, and Memory use the 0–5 HaGRID number classifier. Copy the Pose uses your separate six-gesture model. The results come from the camera, not simulated answers.

🛡️ PRIVACY & LIMITATIONS

Camera frames go to your local Python server and aren't saved by the game. Math and Memory have gentle timers with unlimited retries and no lost stars. Adult assistance is recommended.
