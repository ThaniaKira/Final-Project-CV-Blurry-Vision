BEST CAMERA SETUP

Use bright light from in front of you, move your hand farther from the lens, show your palm and wrist, and hold still before checking.

WHAT THE GAME CHECKS

Number model: 1–5 finger poses, with the same HaGRID-trained weights as your project. Missed hand detections do not count as wrong answers.
